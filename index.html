What is GFC Hair Treatment and how does it work?

Growth Factor Concentrate (GFC) therapy is a highly concentrated, non-surgical treatment derived from your own blood. It releases essential growth factors directly into hair roots to stop hair fall and trigger natural hair regrowth.

How many sessions are required for Acne Scar treatment?

The number of sessions depends on scar depth and type. Typically, 3 to 5 sessions combining Microneedling RF, Subcision, and Chemical Peels yield significant, permanent smoothing.
